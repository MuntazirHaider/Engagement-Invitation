Dress Code

ELEGANT FORMAL ATTIRE

◆

Guests are requested to dress in
sophisticated formal wear.

Men

Tailored suits, tuxedos,
or dinner jackets.

Women

Elegant gowns or
chic evening ensembles.

Jodhpur experiences 24°C – 33°C with occasional monsoon showers.
Please pack for both sunshine and light rain.

With plenty of dancing planned, we suggest comfortable footwear.

The suggested attire is purely inspiration — please wear what makes you feel most comfortable.
