Dress Code

ELEGANT FORMAL ATTIRE

Guests are requested to dress in
sophisticated formal wear.

Men

Tailored suits, tuxedos,
or dinner jackets.

Women

Elegant gowns or
chic evening ensembles.

Jodhpur – experiences temperatures ranging between 24°C and 33°C
with occasional monsoon showers.

Kindly pack accordingly for both sunshine and light rain.

With plenty of dancing and festivities planned, we suggest choosing
comfortable footwear that will carry you effortlessly through the celebrations.

The suggested attire is intended purely as inspiration.
Please feel free to dress in whatever makes you feel most comfortable.
